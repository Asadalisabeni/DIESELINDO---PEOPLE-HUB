# Phase 9 — Operations runbook

Before enabling Overtime for a legal entity, HR Operations must confirm employee-account links, current manager hierarchy, active managed entity access for HR and Payroll, effective employment history, work schedules, branch holiday calendars, and at least one approved rule for every day type the entity uses. HR/Legal must sign the source, effective date, segments, rounding, maximum, eligibility, meal, and transport policy. A government-classified method is only metadata until coefficients have been verified against current official sources and approved. Never copy example JSON from the form into business use without that review.

Daily operations begin by checking pending manager actions before planned starts. Late approvals must be rejected and resubmitted through an authorized exception process rather than edited in the database. After attendance normalization or correction is final, HR reviews comparison evidence. Missing, incomplete, or anomalous Attendance must be corrected in Attendance first. Payroll then confirms only HR-validated eligible units into a valid period. Queue workers must be running for bilingual mail/database notifications, and failed jobs must be investigated without retrying business transactions manually.

Reconcile request counts by status and totals for planned, approved, actual, payable, and payroll-eligible minutes per legal entity and period. Export is scoped and audited; reasons and notes are intentionally absent. Monitor duplicate fingerprints, missing rules, manager fallback frequency, delayed actual validation, zero payable calculations, and requests not included in a period. Backup and restore rehearsal must cover Overtime, generic Approval, Attendance references, notifications, and audit logs. Retention, legal hold, deletion/anonymization, correction/supersession, and evidence reconciliation remain configurable production controls. Database upgrades use additive migration only; never use `migrate:fresh`, truncate, direct status edits, or deletion to repair production records.
