# Phase 10 exit review

Status: implementation and review candidate. Phase 10 is not locked, promoted, or tagged until the Project/UAT Lead reviews the draft pull request, checks the stated production gates, and gives an explicit lock instruction.

## Delivered candidate

The candidate delivers ten additive payroll tables, effective-dated salary components and payroll memberships, confidential maker-checker salary history, configurable payroll groups and periods, exact four-decimal arithmetic, joiner/leaver and mid-period version proration, versioned gross-to-net runs, immutable employee and item snapshots, encrypted bank evidence, approved unpaid-leave and Phase 9 overtime allowance inputs, bilingual validation findings, legal-entity scoping, least-privilege roles, read-only audit access, and an audited register export. The UI states the Phase 11 boundary instead of displaying fabricated statutory results.

## Verification policy

The candidate must pass Pint, Larastan/PHPStan level 8 without suppression, focused Phase 10 feature and security tests, the complete regression suite, Blade compilation, route inventory, a Vite production build, Composer security audit, npm audit, and an additive real-MySQL migration. Browser smoke verification must confirm guest protection, Indonesian and English rendering, dark mode, a 375x812 viewport without horizontal overflow, and a clean warning/error console. Git scope and secret audits must confirm that no credentials, full bank data, confidential salary reasons, or unrelated project files are included.

## Open production gates

HR and Finance must approve component ownership, salary migration, payroll group membership, proration, cutoffs, payment dates, rounding, unpaid leave, and overtime input handling. Phase 11 must verify current PPh 21, BPJS, and overtime wage rules against authoritative official sources and store them as effective-dated configuration before any statutory claim. Independent payroll review, final approval, lock and controlled reopen, bank file, accounting journal, payslip, reporting, retention, disaster recovery, observability, accessibility, performance, and real-role UAT remain open. Locking Phase 10 will establish a reviewed source baseline only; it will not mean regulatory or production readiness.
