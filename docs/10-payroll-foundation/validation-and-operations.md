# Validation and operations

Calculation records explicit findings rather than silently skipping unsafe evidence. Blocking findings cover an empty payroll group, missing effective employment, no payable days, missing independently approved salary, missing or unverified bank data, duplicate bank ownership inside the run, current incomplete or anomalous attendance, payroll-blocked attendance, and negative net pay. Warnings cover a missing unpaid-leave policy, missing overtime allowance components, pending overtime wage rules, pending PPh 21/BPJS rules, and zero net pay. Finding details are encrypted and the displayed message is resolved from a bilingual allowlisted translation key.

A run changes from `draft` to `calculated` only once. Validation requires `calculated` status and refuses the transition while any error finding exists. Warnings are permitted because the Phase 10 boundary intentionally records statutory work for Phase 11; operators must not interpret a warning-bearing validation as authorization to pay. A failed run is never repaired by editing its employee rows, items, findings, or totals. Source data is corrected through its owning workflow, and a new version is created from the period while the old snapshot remains evidence.

Before calculation, operators should verify entity and group, period and cutoff dates, employee membership, employment dates, approved salary versions, verified and unique bank accounts, current normalized attendance, approved unpaid leave, and payroll-eligible overtime inputs. After calculation, reconcile employee count, gross, deductions, employer cost, net, every error and warning, checksums, and differences against the previous version. CSV export is audited and must be stored only in an approved restricted location.

Production operations require a documented maker-checker roster, incident and rollback procedure, source freeze window, reconciliation thresholds, variance sign-off, backup and restore proof, retention and purge approval, and monitoring for failed jobs and unauthorized exports. Phase 10 does not authorize a payment, accounting journal, payslip, regulatory return, or bank instruction. Those outputs remain gated by subsequent reviewed phases.
